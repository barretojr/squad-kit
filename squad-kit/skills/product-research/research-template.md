# Product research — <domain/product>

## User/domain evidence

State the field research actually done, the internal artifacts consulted and the limitations. Never describe opinion as an interview.

## Observed market patterns

For each product: observed pattern/journey, implication, and whether to adopt, adapt or discard.

## Software conventions

Onboarding/login, navigation, search, states, accessibility, trust/security and integrations relevant to this domain.

## Opportunity and scope

Jobs to be done, evidence-based personas, MVP and exclusions.

## API and integration research

Official docs, auth, limits, approvals, webhooks and policies for each external service.

## Credentials and configuration

Where credentials live and how the app behaves without them.

## Sources

Direct link, owner, access date and the claim it supports. Keep facts apart from inference.

## What was done / open items

During research, describe the searches and pages actually consulted. List interviews/field work not done as open items.
